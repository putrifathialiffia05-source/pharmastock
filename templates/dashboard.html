Inventory Control Logic
Formulas
1. Minimum Stock Level (Reorder Point)
Min Stock = (ADC × Lead Time) + Safety Stock

Ensures new stock arrives precisely as inventory touches safety buffer, eliminating stockouts.

2. Maximum Stock Level
Max Stock = Min Stock + (ADC × 14 Days)

Limits capital lockup and prevents medications from languishing past their expiration dates.

3. FEFO Dispensing Principle

First-Expired, First-Out: Batches with the nearest expiration date are automatically selected during dispensing, regardless of arrival order.
